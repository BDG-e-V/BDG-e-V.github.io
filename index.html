Mission Statement

BDG e. V. is an independent, non-partisan non-profit association (eingetragener Verein) dedicated to promoting public and vocational education in the field of information technology, computer science, and software development.

Our mission is to foster digital literacy, support individuals in acquiring practical technical skills, promote knowledge sharing within the software engineering community, and encourage open collaboration and open-source contributions.

Our Programs & Activities

In accordance with our non-profit educational purpose, BDG e. V. carries out the following programs and services:

  • Developer Meetups & Technical Talks:
    Organizing regular meetups, presentations, and technical lectures in Berlin covering software engineering topics, cloud architecture, system design, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and modern programming tools.
  • Hands-on Workshops & Coding Sessions:
    Conducting practical workshops and study groups aimed at vocational training, skill improvement, and hands-on coding experience for students, junior developers, and career switchers.
  • Hackathons & Open-Source Projects:
    Hosting collaborative hackathons and developing public open-source software projects to encourage teamwork, community problem solving, and public digital goods.
  • Educational Mentoring & Community Partnerships:
    Cooperating with educational institutions, community groups, and non-profit initiatives to provide technical mentoring and help aspiring engineers enter the technology sector.
