About Us

BDG e. V. is an independent, non-partisan non-profit association dedicated to promoting public and vocational education in the field of information technology.

What We Do

  • Organizing regular meetups, workshops, and technical talks in Berlin.
  • Hosting hackathons and developer conferences.
  • Collaborating with educational institutions, associations, and tech communities.
